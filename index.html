FAQ

Good questions

no question is too small

Here's what to expect when working with me, from the first message to launch. Don't see your question? Get in touch.

What services do you offer?

I build responsive websites and web apps. That includes turning Figma designs into clean HTML, CSS and JavaScript, building interfaces with React and Tailwind CSS, and developing full-stack features with Laravel and PHP. I also work with Shopify stores, and I use AI tools and prompt engineering to speed up development and automate repetitive tasks.

Do you work with clients outside Bangladesh?

Yes. I work remotely with clients worldwide, using email, chat and video calls to keep projects moving across time zones.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short brief is enough: your goals, your target audience, and any designs, branding or reference sites you already have. If you're still at the idea stage, share examples you like and we'll shape the structure together.

How long does a website project take?

It depends on the scope. A single landing page takes far less time than a multi-page site or a full web application. Once we've gone through your requirements, I'll give you a realistic timeline before any work begins.

Will my website work on mobile and be SEO-friendly?

Yes. I build mobile-first, responsive layouts with semantic HTML, fast load times and good accessibility, which gives your site a solid technical SEO foundation. Rankings also depend on your content and competition, so I can't promise a position, but I make sure nothing technical holds you back.
